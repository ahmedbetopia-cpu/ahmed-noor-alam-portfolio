# Enterprise Multi-Layer Approval Management System

## Executive Summary

Large organizations with multi-tiered management hierarchies frequently suffer from slow decision-making bottlenecks. This case study explores how we engineered an enterprise-ready approval governance engine using Microsoft Power Platform and Microsoft Teams.

---

## The Problem & Challenges

Prior to this solution, the organization operated on physical forms and asynchronous email approvals. 

Key pain points included:
- **Long Turnaround Times**: Average requisition approval cycle was 9.4 business days.
- **Approver Bottlenecks**: If a manager went on leave, the request was stuck indefinitely with no delegation fallback.
- **Audit Deficits**: Compliance auditors had to manually match paper signatures against email PDF attachments.

---

## Architectural Approach

We architected a solution built entirely on Microsoft 365 services to minimize licensing overhead while delivering enterprise reliability.

```
| 1. Request Creation (Power Apps Canvas App)            |

+--------------------------------------------------------+
                           |
                           v

+--------------------------------------------------------+
                           |
                           v

| 4. Governance & Logging (Dataverse & Audit Tables)     |
+--------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Innovative 5-Action Decision Matrix

Rather than limiting managers to binary "Approve" or "Reject" choices, we introduced:
1. **Approve**: Move to subsequent threshold step.
2. **Reject**: Terminate process with required rejection rationale.
3. **Return**: Send back to originator for clarification or invoice re-attachment.
4. **Vet**: Request an advisory technical review from legal or finance without losing queue placement.
5. **Insert Approver**: Dynamically add an ad-hoc stakeholder into the workflow chain.

---

## Key Results & Impact

- **82% Acceleration**: Average turnaround dropped from 9.4 days to under 24 hours.
- **Zero Data Loss**: 100% of audit logs and digital signatures are centralized and exportable.
- **High User Adoption**: 94% of approvals are now completed directly inside Microsoft Teams mobile & desktop clients.
