# Real-Time Sales Performance & Executive Analytics Platform

## Executive Summary

Providing executive leadership with instant visibility into revenue, target variance, and channel velocity is key to competitive agility. This project transformed a delayed monthly reporting cycle into a live, interactive data intelligence dashboard.

---

## The Problem

- **Disparate Data Sources**: Point of Sale (POS) figures lived in SQL, CRM data lived in Salesforce, and operational targets lived in Excel.
- **Reporting Delay**: Business leaders operated with a 15-20 day data lag.
- **No Self-Service**: Custom ad-hoc queries required IT database engineers to write one-off SQL reports.

---

## Solution Architecture

1. **ETL Pipelines in Microsoft Fabric**: Consolidated and cleansed raw ERP transaction logs on an automated 4-hour schedule.
2. **Optimized Star Schema**: Structured fact tables around orders and line items, linked to clean dimensions (`Dim_Region`, `Dim_Product`, `Dim_SalesRep`, `Dim_Calendar`).
3. **Advanced DAX Metrics**: Formulated dynamic measures for YTD growth, YoY comparative variances, and moving averages.
4. **Row-Level Security**: Allowed regional managers to view only their assigned branch metrics while executive leadership maintained national overview.

---

## Business Impact

- **Instant Refresh**: Data updates refreshed 6 times daily automatically.
- **Time Reclaimed**: Saved finance and analytics teams 40+ hours per month of manual aggregation.
- **Strategic Agility**: Enabled regional managers to address stock outages and sales dips mid-quarter rather than post-mortem.
