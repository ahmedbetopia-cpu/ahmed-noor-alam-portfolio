# Barcode-Driven Warehouse Inventory Tracking Application

## Overview

Efficient supply chain operations hinge on accurate, real-time inventory counts. This project digitized physical clipboard counting into a mobile-first Power App equipped with device camera barcode scanning.

---

## The Challenge

- **Manual Data Entry**: Warehouse technicians recorded pallet serials by hand and re-typed them into desktop Excel sheets at the end of the shift.
- **Stock Discrepancies**: Physical stock frequently diverged from system records by up to 8%.
- **Emergency Stockouts**: Procurement was only notified of stock exhaustion after production lines were halted.

---

## Technical Solution

1. **Power Apps Mobile Client**: Built with large touch targets, offline data caching for dead-zones, and native camera barcode decoding.
2. **Instant Validation**: Scanned barcodes are validated instantly against Dataverse product catalogs with auditory and haptic feedback.
3. **Automated Buffer Monitoring**: A lightweight Power Automate cloud flow continuously checks minimum reorder thresholds and notifies purchasing agents via Microsoft Teams cards.

---

## Impact

- **90% Logging Speed Improvement**: Stock receipt logging dropped from 4 minutes to 20 seconds per pallet.
- **Zero Critical Production Halts**: Automated alerts prevented stock exhaustion across two consecutive manufacturing quarters.
